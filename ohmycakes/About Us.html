Our history

Oh My Cake! was founded is 2014 by Naseeha. Her love for sugar paste art changed into a full-time passion while she was finishing off her masters in medical science. She perfected her decorating skills through YouTube and self-teaching. After winning her entrepreneurship competition, she was convinced that that was a sign to become a baker and launch OMC!.The business has evolved so much that the baking team consists of 25 members as well as decorators. OMC produces many cakes, cupcakes and macarons on a daily basis. The company encourages empowerment and uplifting women from the community through skills development. Naseeha's domestic helper was the first employee.

Mission

At OMC, we make cakes that look like craftwork and taste like dessert. Our mission is to add meaning to the lives of every child, couple, grandpa and grandma and best friend. We are here to make your birthday, anniversary, graduation or any milestones achieved very memorable. At OMC every cake tells a story which captures emotions, milestones and great memories. We believe that joy is best shared over something sweet. Our mission is to make cakes that are going to bring people together.
